This notice explains processing of Mondrian contribution-authorization records and does not amend the CLA grants. Reading it is not consent; any legally required separate consent must be obtained separately.

The project controller is 王晓亮 (WANG, XIAOLIANG), contact@shaloong.com. Necessary account identifiers, contact details, agreement versions, assent records and contribution associations are processed for attribution, authority verification, license administration and dispute evidence. 项目依据适用法律允许的合同订立与履行、法定义务或同意等处理依据开展相应处理，不以接受 CLA 作为对所有个人信息处理的概括同意。

Processing relies on the applicable lawful basis, such as contract formation and performance, legal obligations or consent. Acceptance of the CLA is not blanket consent to all personal-data processing.

## 公开、保存和权利 / Disclosure, retention and rights

公开仓库会展示公开提交的署名、账户及 CLA 检查状态；不要在 PR 中填写私人证明材料。项目不公开签署记录导出文件或组织授权文件，访问限于许可管理及依法必要的范围。保存期限以许可管理、争议举证和法定义务所必需为限；不再必要的信息依法删除或匿名化，不承诺所有记录永久保存，也不承诺撤回同意即删除仍依法需要的证据。

Public repositories display public attribution, accounts and CLA status; do not post private evidence in PRs. The project does not publish exported signing records or organizational authority documents and restricts access to license administration and lawful necessity. Retention is limited to the needs of license administration, dispute evidence and legal obligations. Unnecessary information is lawfully deleted or anonymized; neither universal permanent retention nor automatic deletion of legally necessary evidence upon consent withdrawal is promised.

可通过 contact@shaloong.com 申请查询、复制、更正、依法删除、撤回同意或提出其他法定权利请求；处理请求时只核实必要身份信息。对 SAP 持有的数据，可通过 privacy@sap.com 按其声明提出请求；GitHub 数据按其声明办理。撤回同意不影响此前合法处理，也不当然撤销有效知识产权许可。

Request access, copies, correction, lawful deletion, consent withdrawal or other statutory rights at contact@shaloong.com; only necessary identity verification will be requested. Contact privacy@sap.com for SAP-held data under its notice, and use GitHub's notice for its processing. Withdrawal does not invalidate earlier lawful processing or automatically revoke effective intellectual-property grants.

You may contact the project by email to execute the CLA instead. Email also involves data processing and is not represented as necessarily confined to mainland China. A contribution may remain unmerged if necessary authorization information is unavailable.
