# Mondrian Code of Conduct / 社区行为准则

Everyone participating in Mondrian should be able to contribute without
harassment or discrimination, regardless of experience, identity, disability,
background, or personal circumstances. This applies to issues, pull requests,
reviews, project discussions and events, and private interactions arising from
participation in the project.

所有参与者都应能在不受骚扰或歧视的环境中贡献。本准则适用于 Issue、PR、
代码审查、项目讨论和活动，以及因参与项目而产生的私人交流。

## Expected behavior / 期望行为

- Discuss the work respectfully and explain disagreements with evidence.
- Welcome newcomers, answer questions constructively, and accept corrections.
- Respect privacy, accessibility needs, and different language backgrounds.
- Take responsibility for submissions and avoid flooding others with repetitive
  requests or unverified claims, including generated material.

## Unacceptable behavior / 不可接受行为

Harassment, discriminatory abuse, sexual attention without consent, threats,
intimidation, deliberate disruption, doxxing, and retaliation against someone
who reports a concern are unacceptable. Technical disagreement, a rejected
proposal, or criticism of a design is not itself misconduct.

## Reporting and handling / 举报与处理

Report concerns privately to **contact@shaloong.com**, using the subject
`[Mondrian Conduct]`. Include links or relevant context and tell us if you need
your identity withheld. Do not post someone else's private information publicly.
GitHub's **Report content** option can also be used when available; reports to
GitHub and reports to project administrators have different recipients.

Maintainers acknowledge reports as soon as practical, restrict access to those
handling the case, hear relevant parties, and explain the decision privately.
Confidentiality is limited to what is necessary for investigation, enforcement,
and legal obligations. A person named in a report must not decide their own case.
If all project contacts have a conflict, use
[GitHub's abuse reporting channel](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/acceptable-use-policies/github-acceptable-use-policies#reporting-abuse)
for conduct on GitHub; an independent project appeal contact has not yet been
established.

Depending on severity and recurrence, responses may include a requested
correction, a warning, content removal, restricted participation, or a
temporary or permanent ban. Severe threats or harassment can warrant immediate
restrictions. Affected participants may request reconsideration by email with
new information; an uninvolved maintainer should review it when available.

维护者按行为的严重程度和重复情况采取纠正、警告、内容移除、限制参与或封禁。
被举报者不得裁决涉及自己的案件；可通过邮件提出复核并提供新信息。

This is a project-specific policy informed by the
[Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
The [governance document](GOVERNANCE.md) identifies current responsibility and
the limitations of the present maintainer structure.
